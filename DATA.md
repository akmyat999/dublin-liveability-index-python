# Data (not included)
Download each dataset from its provider and place the files next to the script. Project all layers to ITM (EPSG:2157).

| Dataset | Provider | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| OpenStreetMap shapefiles (`gis_osm_*` layers) | Geofabrik, ireland-and-northern-ireland | ODbL 1.0, © OpenStreetMap contributors |
| Small Areas 2022 boundaries | Central Statistics Office (CSO), Census 2022 | See CSO terms |

Required column: `SA_GUID_2022` (Small Area ID). OSM layers used: pois, transport, landuse, roads.
